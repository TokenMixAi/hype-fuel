# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Criticise code, not people. Assume the person you are talking to is acting in good faith
until you have real reason to think otherwise.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion technical and specific. "This reverts when the oracle is stale" is useful.
  "This is terrible" is not.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and that a contributor may disagree with a review.
  Both are normal.
- Respect the private disclosure process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Publishing an exploit
  against a live contract holding other people's money is not research, whatever the intent.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sustained disruption, or publishing someone's private
information.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to chase@manning.dev. Reports stay confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts, in proportion to what
happened. Deliberate bad faith, and anything that puts users' funds at risk, will not get a warning
first.
